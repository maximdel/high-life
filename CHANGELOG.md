# Changelog

## 1.2.0

- Original sound design for everything (all synthesized, no samples): lighter flick, inhale, exhale, coughs, bong bubbles, pill, snort and balloon sounds.
- Ecstasy plays a 30-second, 128 BPM techno loop that only you hear. The screen pulse is synced to the kick drum.
- Heartbeat sounds: racing on speed or cocaine, slow and heavy on the comedown.
- Ketamine has a droning hum, nitrous has the "wah-wah-wah" sound, and salvia has a deep rumble.
- Trips use whispers, chimes and whooshes instead of vanilla mob sounds. DMT adds "machine elf" chirps.
- Hallucinated mobs that only the tripping player sees: floating pigs, a rainbow sheep, fish swimming through the air, parrots, a creeper that is never really there, and allays and vexes circling you on DMT.
- Munchies: food restores extra hunger while you're High.
- Fixed: trip particles and sounds no longer play for players who aren't tripping.

## 1.1.0

- New: salvia (crop, smoked in the bong), LSD (from ergot found on wheat), speed, ecstasy, ketamine, nitrous balloons and DMT (smoked in the bong).
- Every drug has its own screen effect, camera effect and gameplay effect, for example animals fall in love on ecstasy and nearby mobs glow on LSD.
- Chat effects: what you type changes depending on what you're on.
- Speed and cocaine block sleeping. Speed and ecstasy end in a comedown.
- The bong smokes ground cannabis, dried salvia or DMT, and your off hand picks which.

## 1.0.0

- Cannabis: crop, buds, drying, grinder, joints (from Smoke Break cigarettes or paper), bong, cannabutter and pot brownies with a delayed kick-in.
- Smoke Break integration: light joints with its lighter, and fill any of its vapes with Cannabis Distillate e-liquid.
- Magic mushrooms (world generation and spreading) and mushroom tea.
- Tobacco crop that crafts into Smoke Break cigarettes.
- Coca crop and cocaine, with a comedown and overdose.
- Effects: High, Tripping, Wired, Comedown, with screen tints and camera sway.
